About Us

Built to grow.

Lockwood Capital acquires and operates aerospace and defense businesses over a generational time horizon. Our 30-year hold period allows us to focus on what actually matters: building healthy, durable businesses by investing in capabilities, people, and products that compound value over decades.

That patience unlocks opportunity. We fund product development, new market entries, and capabilities that may take years to pay off. Our founder built growth strategies and investment cases at McKinsey & Company for clients ranging from the largest defense primes to PE-backed defense technology companies — giving us the perspective to identify opportunity in complexity.

People are also at the core of what we do. Our founder spent years as a middle school science teacher and soccer coach before entering the investment world. People development is a core value creation lever, and we build environments where our teams are engaged, challenged, and supported.

Our Approach

How we build value

We are hands-on operators who draw on deep A&D experience to grow our businesses. Our playbook is built around six value creation levers.

Product Portfolio Optimization

We map markets, identify white space, and invest in product development opportunities that align with where the defense landscape is headed. Our founder's experience building product roadmaps and growth strategies for defense firms of all sizes means we know how to separate signal from noise — and put capital behind the right bets.

People Development

We create training programs, build promotion pathways, and address organizational bottlenecks through targeted hiring and internal development. Great businesses run on great teams, and we invest in ours accordingly.

Project Investment

Every business has a backlog of positive-NPV projects that were deferred due to cash flow constraints or long payback periods. We identify those opportunities and fund them. Our 30-year horizon means we can invest in projects that others would pass on.

Sector Expertise

Our founder spent years at McKinsey & Company advising across the defense landscape — from the largest primes to emerging defense technology companies. We bring that network, pattern recognition, and strategic perspective to every business we operate.

Operational Investment

We modernize the tools and systems that allow employees to increase their leverage. That includes upgrading legacy infrastructure, streamlining workflows, and deploying AI tools where they create real productivity gains.

Long-Term View

We don't optimize for quarterly results. Our generational hold period allows us to make decisions that compound over decades — investing in capabilities, people, and products that build durable, healthy businesses.

Meet the Founder

Chester Pennock

Chester founded Lockwood Capital with a conviction that the best defense businesses are built by patient, hands-on operators — not financial engineers.

Before launching Lockwood, Chester was an engagement manager at McKinsey & Company, where he built growth strategies, product roadmaps, and investment cases for defense clients ranging from the largest primes to venture- and PE-backed defense technology companies. He holds an MBA with a concentration in finance from Northwestern's Kellogg School of Management and a BA in Economics from Emory University, where he played varsity soccer and served as a teaching assistant in cell biology.

Chester's path to investing was unconventional. He grew up in a town of 1,000 people in North Carolina and spent a year living in Puebla, Mexico as a child. After Emory, he joined Teach For America and taught middle school science in Prince George's County, Maryland, where he also coached the soccer team to a county championship. That experience — developing young people, building culture, and finding ways to win with limited resources — remains central to how he operates today.

Investment Criteria

What we look for

We take a flexible approach to acquisitions. No opportunity will check every box — we use these criteria as a lens to evaluate fit.

Business Profile

  • EBITDA up to $2M is ideal
  • Businesses within the defense supply chain, defined as supporting U.S. or international defense and civil agency programs
  • Clear, value-creating, unfunded project opportunities
  • Open to a wide range of ownership and transaction structures
  • Turnaround opportunities considered

Strategic Fit with Tech Comm

  • Signals intelligence software
  • DF/COMINT subsystem providers
  • Antenna portfolios
  • Adjacent EW and sensing capabilities
  • Complementary defense electronics businesses
